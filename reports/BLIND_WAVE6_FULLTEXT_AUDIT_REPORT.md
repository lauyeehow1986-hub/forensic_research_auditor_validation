## 3. The Vision-Language Model (VLM): What It Solves vs. Fundamental Epistemic Limits

A central question in scientific integrity auditing is: **"Does a Vision-Language Model (VLM) solve the limitations of text- and table-based auditing?"**

The answer is **nuanced and scientifically definitive**: The VLM explicitly solves several critical operational bottlenecks, but cannot overcome fundamental mathematical boundaries without raw microdata.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        WHAT THE VLM & VISION ENGINE SOLVES                             │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Narrative / Chart-Only Papers ("No Table")    │ Digitizes bar heights into vectors  │
│ 2. Optical Deception: SE Whiskers Drawn as SD    │ Catches geometric ratio vs. N       │
│ 3. P-Value Significance Inversions on Brackets   │ Recalculates Welch t-test vs. '***' │
│ 4. Impossible Negative Physical Concentrations   │ Identifies negative zero-bound bars │
│ 5. Cloned Western Blots & Reused Microscopy      │ SIFT/RANSAC & NCC image matching    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┤
│                    WHAT VLM CANNOT SOLVE (FUNDAMENTAL LIMITS)                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. The "Excel Reality" (Formula Fabrication)     │ Bars match fake numbers perfectly   │
│ 2. Missing Sample Size N on Figure / Legend      │ Modular divisibility has no divisor │
│ 3. Continuous Assays Without Step Size           │ Optical densities have no scale     │
│ 4. Novel Text-to-Image AI Diffusion Deepfakes    │ No cloned source panel to match     │
│ 5. Governance Fraud & Fake Peer Review Rings     │ Leaves zero imprint in pixels       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### What the VLM and Vision Engine Explicitly Solves:

1. **The "No Table / Narrative Chart" Bottleneck:**
   - In earlier waves, papers presenting experimental outcomes solely as bar charts or scatter plots without HTML/XML `<table>` tags were unextractable.
   - The VLM (`audit_chart_figure`, `transcribe_and_pipe_to_table_math` with RapidOCR / Surya OCR + Gemini 3.8 Flash / Qwen2.5-VL) digitizes pixel coordinates into exact numerical vectors (mean bar height, dispersion whisker height, group name, caption $N$) and pipes them directly into Gate 3 FastMCP math sieves.
2. **The "Standard Error Labeled as Standard Deviation" Visual Deception ($	ext{LR} = 0.15$):**
   - Authors often plot Standard Error ($SE = SD / \sqrt{n}$) to make error whiskers visually compact, but claim in the caption that error bars represent Standard Deviation ($SD$).
   - A text parser accepts "Data are Mean ± SD". The VLM measures the physical pixel ratio of bar height to whisker height, compares it against the reported sample size $N$, and immediately flags the optical distortion.
3. **P-Value Significance Inversions on Visual Brackets:**
   - In figures, authors frequently draw brackets with `*** (p < 0.001)` over comparison groups with heavily overlapping error bars.
   - The VLM transcribes the two bars and their whiskers, runs Welch's $t$-test recalculation, and flags instances where the visual bracket claims high significance despite Welch's $p > 0.10$.
4. **Physiologically Impossible Negative Error Bar Truncations:**
   - Concentrations of biological molecules (e.g., serum troponin, cytokines) cannot be negative.
   - Fabricators drawing error bars often draw symmetric whiskers extending into negative territory (e.g., $	ext{Mean} = 1.0$, whisker extends down to $-0.8$). The VLM detects error whiskers crossing the $Y=0.0$ baseline on non-negative physical scales.
5. **Multi-Panel Western Blot Splicing & Microscopy Panel Duplication:**
   - Gate 3.5's Lean ImageTwin engine uses Normalized Cross-Correlation (NCC > 0.95) for gel bands, SIFT + RANSAC homography for rotated/flipped microscopy fields, and DuckDB SIMD vectors for cross-paper reuse.

### What the VLM CANNOT Solve (Inherent Epistemic Boundaries):

1. **The "Excel Reality" (Formula-Generated Cohorts):**
   - If an author fabricates 100 fake patient records in Excel and plots them into a bar chart, the bar height and error whisker *accurately reflect the fabricated spreadsheet rows*.
   - The VLM transcribes the chart with 100% pixel perfection; the math engine verifies that the bar height equals the mean and the whisker equals the standard deviation. The data is internally consistent; only raw patient-level CSV microdata (Carlisle ANOVA, covariance positive semi-definiteness) can detect the fabrication.
2. **Missing Sample Size ($N$):**
   - If the author omits sample size $N$ from the figure axis, legend, and text, the VLM cannot guess whether $N=6$ or $N=60$. Without $N$, modular divisibility (GRIM) cannot execute.
3. **Continuous Biological Variables Without Known Instrument Step Size:**
   - Continuous physical quantities (e.g., optical density, fluorescence intensity) have analog distributions. GRIM cannot be applied without knowing the exact analog-to-digital measurement step size of the laboratory instrument.
4. **Diffusion-Generated Synthetic Figures (Text-to-Image AI):**
   - If a paper-mill synthesizes a novel Western blot or microscopy field from scratch using Stable Diffusion, there is no duplicated source panel for SIFT/RANSAC or pHash to match against. Detecting generative diffusion requires raw TIFF sensor-level noise residuals (`TruFor Noiseprint++`), not high-level semantic vision.
5. **Governance, Authorship, and Peer-Review Fraud:**
   - Fake reviewers, paper-mill authorship brokering, and forged ethics approvals contain no visual or arithmetic elements.
